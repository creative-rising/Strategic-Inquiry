Who gets to define the problem?

Before deciding what AI should do, examine how the situation is defined, who has influence over it, and what other responsible choices may be available.

A coaching-informed Strategic Inquiry approach developed by Creative Rising.

Three lenses for inquiry

These lenses support examination before commitment to a solution. They are not a rigid sequence, and the original proposal may still be appropriate after examination.

FRAME

What are we asking?

How is the situation defined? What assumptions shape the question, and what may be outside the frame?

POWER

Who gets to shape it?

Whose knowledge counts? Who interprets, influences, decides, lives with the effects, and answers afterward?

POSSIBILITY

What else becomes possible?

What other questions, approaches or responsible actions might be worth considering?
