# Strategic Inquiry

**FRAME → POWER → POSSIBILITY**

Strategic Inquiry is a coaching-informed approach developed through Creative Rising's work on organizational questions, participation, power, and decisions involving AI.

Before deciding what AI should do, the approach invites us to examine how the situation is defined, who has influence over that definition, and what other responsible choices may be available.

## Three lenses

- **FRAME — What are we asking?** How has the situation been defined? Which assumptions and perspectives are included or left outside the frame?
- **POWER — Who gets to shape it?** Whose knowledge informs interpretation? Who influences options, decides, lives with the consequences, and is accountable?
- **POSSIBILITY — What else becomes possible?** What other questions, approaches, or actions could be worth considering before choosing a solution?

The lenses are not a rigid sequence. The original framing or proposed use of AI may remain appropriate after examination.
